Certification playbook Back to Insights

EIS Bridge connects any POS, ERP, or merchant/business system to the BIR Electronic Invoicing System — without system source-code changes. Merchants complete EIS CERT and Permit to Transmit (PTT) per BIR; the bridge handles mapping, signing, and transmission.

EIS Bridge is not affiliated with or accredited by the BIR. BIR certifies taxpayer systems, not software providers. Tax compliance remains the taxpayer's responsibility.
