Most homes are 3–5 people (54.8% of households)

September 5, 2026 · Day 28 of 60 · PH Findings · 60-Day Series · households, size

Key stat: Households with 3–5 members: 54.8%

Household size distribution clusters in the middle:

  • 3–5 members: 54.8% of households
  • Smaller (1–2) and larger (6+) split the remainder

The "nuclear family with extras" shape — couples, kids, sometimes grandparents — dominates national housing economics.

Promo pack sizes, "family" menu tiers, and data plan sharing assumptions should default to multi-person homes, not single-person condos.

Takeaway: solo urban professional marketing is real in NCR, but it is not 54.8% of homes.
