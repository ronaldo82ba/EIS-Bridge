National average family income: about ₱25,600 a month (2021 FIES)

August 23, 2026 · Day 15 of 60 · PH Findings · 60-Day Series · income, fies

Key stat: Average monthly family income: ~₱25,600 (FIES 2021)

FIES 2021 reports national average monthly family income of about ₱25,600 (2021 prices). That single number is easy to misread.

It is an average, pulled up by high-income tails. It is family income, not per-person or per-worker pay. It is 2021 — before the inflation waves merchants felt in 2022–2024.

Still, it is the official household income anchor PSA publishes. Our spine uses it alongside per capita and cluster cuts, not as a stand-in for "typical salary."

Business reading: if your product needs ₱30k/month from every household, you are already above the national average family income line.
