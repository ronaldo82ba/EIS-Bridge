The ₱100k/month household band is only ~2.9% of persons

August 18, 2026 · Day 10 of 60 · PH Findings · 60-Day Series · income, salary

Key stat: ₱100k+/mo household band: ~2.9% of persons

LinkedIn threads treat ₱100,000/month household income as a common milestone. FIES-based class research says otherwise at the person level:

  • Households in bands approaching ₱100k/month represent only about 2.9% of persons in the PIDS cluster spine

That is not "nobody" — it is millions of people — but it is a thin slice of 112.7M. Most Filipinos live in clusters where monthly family cash flow is a fraction of that figure.

Sim rule: we do not sample agents as if six-figure household income were the default Filipino experience.
